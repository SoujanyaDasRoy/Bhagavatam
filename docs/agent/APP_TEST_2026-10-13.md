## Findings
1. **Splash status bar (fixed).** On the dark splash the clock and battery icons were dark on dark and almost invisible. The splash now forces light status-bar icons.
2. **Dark palette had weak separation (fixed, measured).** Card against page was 1.14:1, divider line 1.47:1, the tinted icon tile 1.09:1 (Sandhya); Ratri was worse (card 1.07:1). New values: Sandhya card 20263A (1.23:1), divider 3B4260 (1.87:1), secondary text B4AE9F (6.8:1 on cards), accent F2B27A; Ratri card 14141A, divider 34343C, secondary 9F9A8D (7.0:1). Primary text stays above 14:1. The cards now read as cards without being brighter.
3. **Reader floating bar (not fixed).** The Listen / previous / next bar floats over the text with no scrim, so verse text shows through behind and around it. Suggest a gradient scrim of the page colour behind the bar.
4. **Theme picker (improved).** The "Dark" preview tile was nearly the same colour as the dark page; the stronger divider colour gives it a visible edge. A 1dp outline in the ink colour at 30% would make it certain.
5. **Home first-run wording.** Fresh install says "Resume reading, Stopped at shloka 1" although nothing was read (covered by the onboarding plan).
6. **Search.** The typo query returned 100 results, which is the cap; the first hits are Sanskrit verses of the Mahatmya, not the reading language. Check that "Did you mean" is shown when the keyboard is closed.
7. **Settings tap right after launch** did not open the tab once (the first tap landed while the app was still drawing); a second tap worked. Not reproducible as a defect.

## Where the colours changed
`ui/theme/Tokens.kt` (AppDark, AppRatri), `ui/theme/Color.kt` (Sandhya, Ratri reader colours), `ui/screens/Splash.kt` (status bar). Light themes untouched.
