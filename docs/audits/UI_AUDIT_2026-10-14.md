## Design review: Bhagavatam (Android, Jetpack Compose)

Reviewed with the apple-design-hig skill (dickwu/apple-design-skill, 123 HIG pages). The app is Android-only, so Apple's principles and foundations apply (accessibility, color, typography, layout, writing, onboarding, settings, dark mode); Apple's platform conventions (tab bars on iOS, sheets) are used only as guidance. Numbers come from `ui/theme/Tokens.kt`, a grep of the source and the screenshots taken on an emulator and a Samsung SM-A556E on 2026-10-14.

### Summary
Rating: **Good**. The app has a point of view that comes from its subject (a manuscript-like reader with Kangra painting, saffron accent, and warm paper and night palettes), the text colours pass contrast in every theme, and the first-run flow is two short steps with no permission asked. The weak points are small type in secondary screens, a few icon buttons below the default touch size, tab bar and player text capped at 130% font scale, and one place where Android-only features are described with iOS wording.

### Critical
None found with the data I could measure.

### Improvements

- **High: text below Apple's 11 pt floor and below the project's own 15 sp rule for Indic text.**
  - **What:** 134 `fontSize` values under 15 sp in `ui/`; 10 sp in the mini player (`Bars.kt:227`) and the player (`Player.kt:303`), 11 sp in the dictionary (`DictionaryScreen.kt:474, 613, 618, 665`), annotation sheet (`AnnotationSheet.kt:311, 317, 336`) and credits (`CreditsScreen.kt:167`).
  - **Why:** `accessibility.md › Vision`: "Use recommended defaults for custom type sizes", default 17 pt, minimum 11 pt. The project's own rule (`CLAUDE.md` section 5) is 15 sp minimum for Indic text.
  - **Fix:** raise those to 12 to 13 sp for Latin metadata and 15 sp or more for any Indic string; the Home and Content type was raised in 1188ffc, the rest was not.

- **High: Dynamic Type is capped.**
  - **What:** `CappedFontScale(max = 1.3f)` wraps the floating tab bar and the mini player (`Bars.kt:80`).
  - **Why:** `accessibility.md › Vision`: "give people the option to enlarge text by at least 200 percent".
  - **Fix:** let the bar grow (taller capsule, two lines) up to 2.0, or stack the labels under icons only above 1.3 and keep text visible.

- **Medium: icon buttons under the default control size.**
  - **What:** 36 dp (`Bars.kt:267` next), 40 dp (`Player.kt:288, 321`, bookmark in `Reader.kt:539`), 44 dp colour swatches (`AnnotationSheet.kt:172`).
  - **Why:** `accessibility.md › Mobility`: default 44 x 44 pt, "spacing between controls [is] as important as size"; the project rule is 48 dp.
  - **Fix:** 48 dp touch area with the same visible icon size.

- **Medium: no explicit Reduce Motion path.** There is no code reading the system animation scale or a reduce-motion setting; the splash glow repeats forever. `accessibility.md › Cognitive`: "When this setting is active, ensure your app ... responds by reducing automatic and repetitive animations". Compose follows the system animator scale for its own animations, but the infinite glow and the staggered splash should be skipped when the scale is 0.

- **Medium: Skandha tiles may not meet 4.5:1.** White on the raw Skandha hue measures 3.0:1 (E07A1F) and cream on it 2.8:1. The code darkens the far gradient stop to reach 4.6:1 (`skGradient`), but the near stop and the mandala overlay were not measured. Sample real pixels from a screenshot before calling it fine.

- **Medium: an app-level appearance switch exists.** The moon button on Home and System/Light/Dark plus four reading themes. `dark-mode.md`: no app-specific appearance switch. A reading app has a real reason for a sepia theme, so keep Pothi, but consider removing the Home moon button and leaving the choice in Settings.

- **Low: 15 icons have `contentDescription = null`.** Most sit inside a labelled row (tab icons, the check mark of the selected language); verify `Bars.kt:202`, `DictionaryScreen.kt:183, 684, 718` and `CreditsScreen.kt:209`, since those may be the only label of a tap target.

### Craft notes
- **Point of view:** strong and rooted in the product. The Kangra painting on the intro, the Devanagari title and the warm paper carry it. The palette (cream paper, terracotta accent, serif titles) is also the look the skill calls the common default; it holds here because the subject is a printed manuscript, but the signature element should stay the painting, not the colour.
- **Typography:** reading text is set in Tiro Devanagari, Noto Serif Bengali and a serif for English and Odia, which gives the Indic text personality; UI chrome is a neutral sans. Odia uses the system serif, which is the one weak face.
- **Remove one accessory:** the mandala line art behind every Skandha tile and story card. The art and colour already identify the tile; the line art adds noise under small text.
- **Boldness in one place:** Home has five full-width cards of near-equal weight (resume, saved and dictionary, reading progress, stories, content) plus Shloka of the day. Make the resume card the only dark, large element and let the rest be quiet surface cards.

### What works
- Contrast: Prabhat text 15.8:1, secondary 5.3:1, gold 6.1:1, accent 5.2:1, button text 5.8:1; Sandhya secondary 8.3:1, gold 9.3:1, Ratri secondary 7.5:1. All pass 4.5:1.
- First run: two steps, no sign-in, no permission prompt, offline stated, `onboarding.md` advice followed.
- Four-item tab bar with text labels; one language setting for the app and one for reading, with a written rule.
- Errors and corrections are inline in the reading flow ("Is it spelled correctly?") and leave the page where it was.

### Platform notes
- **Writing:** "Preview, 1.1.1" on the paath step reads like a version number; "English Transliteration" is the Roman script, not English; "Paath" is not explained for English readers. `writing.md`: say what happens in plain words.
- **Consistency:** labels mix title case ("Your reading") and sentence case ("Show alongside"); pick one.
- **Not measured:** screen reader order and labels (TalkBack was not run), the 200% font size layouts, and Indic text at large sizes. These need a TalkBack and font-scale pass on the phone.
