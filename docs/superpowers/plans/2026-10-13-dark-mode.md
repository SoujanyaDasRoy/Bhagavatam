# Dark mode for a scripture reader: research and plan

**Status:** plan only, nothing implemented. Written 2026-10-13 after the owner said the current dark mode "is too black".

## 1. What the current dark mode is
| Theme | Page | Card | Text | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sandhya (the "Dark" look) | `151412` | `211F1C` | `EDE8DC` | warm charcoal, but still very close to black (luminance 0.007) |
| Ratri (OLED) | `000000` | `12110F` | `E2DDD1` | true black, by design |

Text on Sandhya is 15.1:1 and white on it would be 18.4:1. That is more contrast than a long-reading surface needs, and it is why it feels harsh.

## 2. What the research says (sources at the end)
1. **Do not use pure black as the main surface.** Material Design's dark-theme guidance recommends a dark grey (its example is `#121212`) so that depth can be shown by lighter surfaces, and so that text does not glow. Pure black belongs to an optional OLED mode.
2. **Contrast has a ceiling for comfort, not only a floor.** Material asks for at least 15.8:1 between white text and the base surface so the lightest elevated surface still reaches 4.5:1. That is a floor for accessibility; for long reading, softer text (off-white, not white) on a slightly lifted surface is the usual recommendation.
3. **Desaturate and lighten accents on dark.** Saturated colours "vibrate" on dark backgrounds; Material suggests tones around the 200 value of a palette. The app's saffron accent (`F2B27A`) is already in that range.
4. **Halation.** Bright text on near-black can bloom, which is worse for people with astigmatism. Accessibility sources therefore suggest `#121212` to `#1A1A1A` for backgrounds and `#D4D4D4` to `#E0E0E0` for text, never `#000` with `#FFF`.
5. **The evidence is mixed, so choice matters more than a single answer.** A 2021 IEEE Access study found less visual fatigue in dark mode but a preference for light mode; a 2024 paper calls the evidence contradictory; ambient light matters at least as much as the colour scheme. I found no controlled study of e-reader dark grey versus black, and none on sepia. Treat these as design guidance, not proof.
6. **Reading apps offer several looks, not one.** Libby, for example, offers Light, Sepia and Dark. This app already has four (Prabhat, Pothi, Sandhya, Ratri).

## 3. What this means for this app
- The owner's reaction ("too black") fits the research: a pure-black or near-black page with off-white text is the harshest reading option. The existing Sandhya is warm but still darker than Material's `#121212`.
- A scripture reader is read for long sessions, in Devanagari, Bengali and Roman text, often at night. Comfort for long text matters more than the "OLED black" look.
- The fix is to **lift the dark page to a warm mid-dark grey**, keep a clear step between page and card, and use slightly softer text. True black stays available as the separate Ratri look.

## 4. Proposal
Three dark looks instead of one and a half:

| Look | Page | Card | Divider | Text / secondary | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Sandhya (new default dark)** | `2B2723` | `36322C` | `50493F` | `EDE8DC` / `BDB6A6` | long reading, any room |
| **Dim** (optional, softer) | `302C27` | `3B3731` | `564F45` | `E6E0D2` / `B6AF9F` | reading in a dim room, lowest glare |
| **Ratri** (kept) | `000000` | `12110F` | `36322D` | `E2DDD1` / `9F9A8D` | OLED screens, battery |

Measured contrast for the candidates (WCAG, computed 2026-10-13):

| Page colour | Text on page | Secondary on page | Secondary on card | Saffron accent on card | Gold on card |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `151412` (now) | 15.1 | 9.1 | 8.1 | 8.9 | 8.3 |
| `24211D` | 13.1 | 7.9 | 7.0 | 7.6 | 7.1 |
| `2B2723` (proposed Sandhya) | 12.1 | 7.3 | 6.3 | 6.9 | 6.5 |
| `302C27` (Dim) | 11.3 | 6.9 | 5.9 | 6.4 | 6.0 |

All text pairs stay above the 4.5:1 body-text floor with margin, and above the 7:1 level for the primary text. The card step is 1.16:1 (page to card), so card edges rely on the divider (1.7:1 against the page) as they do today.

## 5. Tasks
1. **Look at it before committing.** Build two debug variants (page `2B2723` and `302C27`) behind a hidden Settings switch, and view Home, Reader, Settings, Search and the Player on the emulator and a real phone in a dark room. The owner picks one. (Colour on a monitor is not colour on a phone.)
2. **Tokens.** Change `AppDark` and the Sandhya reader colours in `Tokens.kt` and `Color.kt`; keep `AppRatri`. If Dim is chosen, add `ReaderTheme.Dim` and an `AppDim` palette, the picker label in the three languages, and the settings row.
3. **Hard-coded dark colours.** Search the UI for colours that assume the old page: gradient overlays and card art backdrops (`ArtBackdrop`), the mini player, the splash, the Home hero (`skGradient`), and the story tiles, which are saturated blues and can look heavy on a lifted page. Check each in dark.
4. **Scrim and bars.** The reader bottom scrim uses the page colour, so it follows automatically; confirm the navigation bar background and the language pill.
5. **Verify.** Contrast table recomputed from the final tokens (script), screenshots of every main screen in Sandhya, Dim and Ratri, font scale 1.3, and a crash check.
6. **Docs.** Update `DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` and the theme names in the three languages.

## 6. Decisions for the owner
- Page colour: `2B2723` (clearly lifted, recommended) or `24211D` (a smaller step from today)?
- Add the separate **Dim** look, or only lift Sandhya?
- Keep Ratri unchanged (true black)?
