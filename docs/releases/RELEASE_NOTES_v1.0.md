# Bhagavatam v1.0.0 Release Notes

**Bhagavatam v1.0.0** is the first official release of the quiet, private, and ad-free scripture reader and listener for the **Shrimad Bhagavat Mahapuran** (*শ্রীমদ্ভাগবত মহাপুরাণ / श्रीमद्भागवत महापुराण*).

Built from the ground up with Jetpack Compose and offline-first principles, this release brings the entire sacred text into the palm of your hand across Sanskrit, Hindi, Bengali, and English.

---

## 🌟 What's New in v1.0.0

### 📖 Complete Scripture Text & Multi-Language Translations
- **All 12 Skandhas + Mahatmya**: Full canonical text comprising **341 Chapters** and **14,580 Verses** based on the revered Gita Press, Gorakhpur editions.
- **Layered Multi-Language Reading**: Read the original Sanskrit shlokas alongside Hindi bhavartha, Bengali translations, and English commentary.
- **Tri-Script Sanskrit**: Render original shlokas in **Devanagari**, **Bengali script** (*শ্রীমদ্ভাগবত*), or **IAST Roman transliteration**.
- **Bengali Typography & Localized Titles**: Full Bengali UI localization with native Bengali numerals and canonical titles rendered in crisp `NotoSerifBengali`.

---

### 🎧 Spotify-Inspired Audio Player & Live Paath
- **Spotify Expanded Player UI**:
  - **Minimal & Functional**: Styled after Spotify's mobile layout with square hero artwork, prominent center play/pause, and thin seekbar scrubber with exact verse and duration counters.
  - **5-Button Transport Bar**: Quick toggle between Chapter / Skandha / Continuous Granth playback (`PlayThrough`), Previous, Hero Play/Pause, Next, and Loop.
  - **Synchronized Karaoke Lyrics**: Live line-by-line Sanskrit recitation highlights and sentence-level translation sync. Tap any line or sentence to immediately jump playback there.
- **Floating Mini Player**:
  - Glassmorphic floating pill with album thumbnail, live chapter progress, and playback controls.
  - **Swipe-to-Dismiss**: Natural horizontal swipe gesture to dismiss the mini player.
  - **Dedicated Close (`✕`) Button**: Stop playback and remove the player session in one tap.

---

### 📺 Zero-Download YouTube Recitation Streams
- **Instant Chapter Streams**: Toggle seamlessly between on-device text-to-speech recitation and authentic YouTube recitation streams for all 12 Skandhas in Hindi, Bengali, English, and Sanskrit.
- **Zero Storage Required**: Enjoy complete chapter recitations without downloading large audio files.
- **Background & External Playback**: Direct integration to continue playback in the background or in the YouTube app.

---

### ✍️ Study, Notes & Search
- **Highlights & Notes**: Select any verse text to highlight in sacred hues, add personal notes, or bookmark verses for daily contemplation. All data stays strictly on your phone.
- **Omni Search**: Search across Sanskrit, Hindi, Bengali, and English simultaneously, or jump straight to any reference (e.g. `10.29.1`).
- **Thematic Index**: Browse famous episodes like *Gajendra Moksha*, *Dhruva Charitra*, *Prahlad Charitra*, and *Rasa Lila*.

---

### 🎨 Themes & Customization
- **Four Sacred Themes**:
  - 🌅 **Prabhat**: Clean, bright daylight theme
  - 📜 **Pothi**: Traditional manuscript sepia theme
  - 🌄 **Sandhya**: Warm twilight sunset theme
  - 🌌 **Ratri**: Pure dark mode for night reading
- **Text Scaling**: Pinch-to-zoom and adjustable line spacing.
- **Keep Screen On & Sleep Timer**: Built-in sleep timer (`15m`, `30m`, `45m`, `60m`) for bedtime listening.

---

## 🔒 Privacy & Offline First
- **No Ads, No Subscriptions, No Accounts**: Pure, undisturbed devotional experience.
- **Zero Tracking**: No analytics, telemetry, or third-party trackers.
- **100% Offline**: The entire text database is bundled inside the app.

---
